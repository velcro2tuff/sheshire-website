How it works

  • 1. Send your request and your occasion.
  • 2. Choose the budget for the item you want us to shop for.
  • 3. Pay the R150 service fee.
  • 4. We review the request and confirm the details with you.
  • 5. We buy the requested item and keep you updated.

  • Payment 1: Service fee is paid first to confirm the order.
